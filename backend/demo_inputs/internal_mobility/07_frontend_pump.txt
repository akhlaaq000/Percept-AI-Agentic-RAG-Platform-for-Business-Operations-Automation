As a Frontend Engineer, you’ll play a foundational role in shaping both our product and our company. You’ll work closely with the founding team, contribute across the stack (especially frontend), and help define our technical direction and culture from the ground up.

This is a rare chance to join something early and make a real impact — we hope Pump becomes a career-defining chapter for everyone who joins.
