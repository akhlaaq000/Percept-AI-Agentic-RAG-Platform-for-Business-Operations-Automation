Responsibilities:

THE ROLE:

As a Senior System Test Engineering Manager within the Product Development Group, you will lead system test engineering efforts that enable AMD’s next‑generation accelerator products from early development through high‑volume manufacturing. This role is responsible for defining system‑level test strategy, driving execution across lab, manufacturing, and customer environments, and ensuring robust coverage for functionality, compatibility, and reliability.

You will manage and mentor a team of system test engineers while working cross‑functionally with design, product engineering, operations, and business partners to ensure products meet quality, schedule, and customer expectations. This role combines technical leadership, people management, and strong execution ownership in a fast‑paced product development environment.

THE PERSON:

You are a collaborative and results‑driven engineering leader with a strong background in system test and product development. You balance technical depth with people leadership, are comfortable guiding teams through complex and ambiguous challenges, and communicate effectively with stakeholders at all levels. You take ownership, proactively remove obstacles, and are motivated by delivering high‑quality products to market through strong team execution.

KEY RESPONSIBILITIES:

Lead and manage a team of system test engineers supporting Instinct accelerator product development
Define and execute system‑level test strategies spanning pre‑silicon planning, post‑silicon bring‑up, customer samples, and high‑volume manufacturing
Drive alignment on test coverage, validation readiness, and quality metrics across lab, manufacturing, and customer environments
Partner cross‑functionally with Design, Product Engineering, Operations, Supply Chain, Business, and Customer Engineering teams
Oversee planning and execution for system test deliverables, schedules, and resource allocation
Identify technical, schedule, and quality risks; drive mitigation plans and escalation as needed
Communicate program and execution status, risks, and recommendations to senior and executive leadership
Collaborate closely with globally distributed teams, including Asia‑based engineering and operations organizations
Champion continuous improvement in test methodologies, tools, processes, and team execution

PREFERRED EXPERIENCE:

Experience leading system test or validation teams in semiconductor product development
Strong background supporting GPU, accelerator, or complex SoC‑based products
Experience with pre‑silicon planning, post‑silicon validation, customer sampling, and manufacturing readiness
Proven people management experience, including hiring, mentoring, and performance development
Experience working in complex, cross‑functional, and geographically distributed engineering environments
Strong written and verbal communication skills, including presenting status and recommendations to senior or director‑level leadership
Proficiency with standard Microsoft tools (e.g., Office, Windows OS) for planning, tracking, and reporting

ACADEMIC CREDENTIALS:

Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Engineering or a related technical discipline preferred
