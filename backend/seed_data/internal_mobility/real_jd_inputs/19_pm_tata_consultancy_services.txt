Core responsibilities
Customer obsession: Work backwards from the customer to identify their problems and create products and features that solve them.
Product strategy: Define the product vision and roadmap, and create detailed requirement specifications.
Technical leadership: Understand the core technologies of the product, communicate technical details effectively to both technical and no n-technical teams, and guide product development.
Cross-functional collaboration: Partner closely with engineering, design, science, and other teams to bring products to life.
Full lifecycle management: Manage the entire product lifecycle, from initial concept through launch, including developing marketing plans, building business models, and analyzing success metrics.
Business and financial acumen: Analyze business impact, dive deep into metrics, and be responsible for revenue and adoption goals.
Key qualifications and skills
Technical experience: A strong technical background, such as software development or network development, is essential.
Product management experience: Experience in full-cycle product management, especially for large-scale SaaS, PaaS, or IaaS products.
Communication: The ability to write concisely and compellingly, and communicate effectively with all stakeholders.
Strategic thinking: The ability to balance technical challenges with strategic goals and maintain a customer-obsessed mindset.
Curiosity: A deep curiosity about technology and a drive to invent on behalf of the customer
