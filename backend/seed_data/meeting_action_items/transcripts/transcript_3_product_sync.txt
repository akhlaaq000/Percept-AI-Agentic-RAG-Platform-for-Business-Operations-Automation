Product Sync — March 5th

Present: Jamie, Priya, and the product team.

The meeting kicked off with a look at the new checkout flow. Jamie shared that the design mockups are mostly done but haven't gone through a proper review yet — a few people on the team have seen bits and pieces informally, but nothing structured. There was a short debate about whether to fold this into the next design review or schedule something dedicated just for checkout. The team landed on folding it into the next design review to keep things moving. Jamie is going to review the checkout flow design mockups one more time and prepare them to share ahead of that review.

Priya then brought up something customer support had flagged — apparently there's been a small but steady stream of complaints about confusing error messages when a payment fails. Support has been fielding the same kinds of questions repeatedly, and it's creating extra ticket volume that could probably be avoided. After some discussion about a few specific examples support had shared, Priya agreed to take a pass at rewriting the payment error messages so they're clearer for customers.

There was also a passing comment from someone about maybe eventually redesigning the whole checkout confirmation page at some point, but nobody committed to it or gave it a timeline — it was more of a "someday" idea than anything concrete, so nothing was formally assigned there.

The team confirmed the current sprint is tracking fine overall, with no other blockers to flag.
